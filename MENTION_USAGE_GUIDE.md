# @Mention Feature - User Guide

## Overview
Mention your friends in posts to notify them and create clickable links to their profiles.

---

## How to Mention Friends

### Step 1: Start Typing Your Post
Navigate to the Social page and click in the "Share Your Progress" text area.

```
Type your message normally...
```

### Step 2: Type the @ Symbol
When you want to mention a friend, type `@`:

```
Great workout today with @
```

**What happens:** A dropdown appears showing all your friends!

### Step 3: Filter Your Friends (Optional)
Continue typing to filter the list:

```
Great workout today with @jo
```

**What happens:** The list filters to show only friends matching "jo":
- John Smith
- Sarah Johnson  
- @johnny

### Step 4: Select Your Friend

**Option A - Keyboard:**
- Press `↓` or `↑` to navigate
- Press `Enter` to select

**Option B - Mouse:**
- Hover over a friend
- Click to select

### Step 5: Continue Typing
After selection, the mention is inserted:

```
Great workout today with @john 
```

The cursor is automatically positioned after the mention with a space, ready for you to continue typing!

## After Posting

Your mention appears as a **blue clickable link**:

```
Great workout today with @john! 💪
                        ^^^^^^
                    (blue link)
```

**What happens:**
1. ✅ Your friend receives a notification
2. ✅ The mention is clickable in the post
3. ✅ Clicking navigates to their profile (when implemented)

## Multiple Mentions

You can mention multiple friends in one post:

```
Amazing run with @sarah and @mike today! 
We crushed 10k together 🏃‍♀️🏃‍♂️
```

**Result:**
- Both Sarah and Mike receive notifications
- Both mentions are clickable links
- Each friend is notified individually

When you mention someone, they receive a notification:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 🔔 Notifications                    │
├─────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 📝 You were mentioned in a post     │
│    Sarah Johnson mentioned you      │

└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Clicking "View Post" takes them directly to your post.

## Privacy & Control

### Who Can Mention You?
- Only your accepted friends can mention you
- Users you've blocked cannot mention you
- Users with privacy settings disabled won't appear in dropdowns

### Managing Mentions
- You can edit posts to add/remove mentions
- Deleting a post removes all mentions
- Mentioned users are notified immediately

---

## Troubleshooting

### "Dropdown doesn't appear when I type @"
- Make sure you have friends added
- Check that you're in the post creation area
- Try refreshing the page

### "I can't find my friend in the dropdown"
- Verify they're in your friends list
- Check if they have a username set
- Try typing more of their name

### "Mention didn't notify my friend"
- Ensure they're still in your friends list
- Check that the post was created successfully
- Verify their notification settings

## FAQ

**Q: Can I mention someone who's not my friend?**
A: No, only your accepted friends appear in the dropdown.

**Q: Will they know I mentioned them?**
A: Yes! They receive a notification immediately.

**Q: Can I edit mentions after posting?**
A: Yes, edit your post to add or remove mentions.

**Q: What if I misspell a username?**
A: Use the dropdown to avoid typos - it inserts the correct username.

**Q: Can I mention multiple people?**
A: Yes! Mention as many friends as you want in one post.

**Q: Do mentions work in comments?**
A: Currently, mentions only work in posts (not comments yet).

**Q: Can I turn off mention notifications?**
A: Notification settings coming soon!
